Executive recommendation

We recommend a phased rollout,
beginning with a pilot, built to prove value early.

A focused first phase, sized to sit inside the high-performance course Tennis Australia already runs. Two further phases follow, with learnings applied throughout.

Pilot

A small, agreed cohort tests the approach against real coaching moments.

Depth

Go deeper and wider as we continue, building on what phase one reveals.

Scale

Adjust the scale to fit Tennis Australia’s needs as we continue.

Practical need

The pilot would focus on moments
coaches encounter every week. Examples below.

A coach helps someone reset after a difficult loss.
Capability to practiseA short reset routine a coach can prompt in the moment, rehearsed before it’s needed.
A coach navigates a difficult conversation with someone they coach, or a parent.
Capability to practiseRehearsing the conversation ahead of time, rather than improvising inside it.
A coach recognises that a concern needs specialist support.
Capability to practiseA clear, appropriate handover into Tennis Australia’s existing referral pathway.
Method

Coaches practise the tools
before applying them with players.

The full ten-pillar framework is in the appendix.

Experience

Coaches try the exercise themselves first, as a participant.

Rehearse

They practise using it inside a tennis-specific scenario.

Review

They discuss how, and whether, it fits their own coaching.

Illustrative example
“Name one moment this week where you paused before reacting. How did that impact your performance?”
